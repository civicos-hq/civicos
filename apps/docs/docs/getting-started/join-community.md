# Join a community

Everything in CivicOS is scoped to a **community** — a state + Local
Government Area (LGA) pair. Issues, petitions, and representatives all
belong to one. Joining a community is what unlocks civic action for that
place.

## Your first communities

When you land on CivicOS for the first time, you'll be walked through two
steps.

**Step 1 — find your communities.** Search by name for whatever you're
looking for: your town, your LGA, or your university. You don't need to
know which local government area anything sits in — searching
"University of Abuja" finds it directly, and so does searching
"Gwagwalada" to see everything in that area council.

You can pick **more than one**. Most people join both where they live and
where they study or work, and that's the intended shape: a student in
Gwagwalada attending the University of Abuja belongs in both.

If you'd rather look around than search, "Browse by state and LGA" opens
the older filter view — useful when you don't know the name of anything
and just want to see what exists near you.

**Step 2 — pick your home.** Of the communities you selected, choose one
as your **home community**. This is a real choice, not a formality: see
the next section for what it decides, and note the 30-day limit on
changing it.

:::tip Several universities in one city?
That's expected, and it's why search comes first. Abuja's universities
sit across three different area councils — the University of Abuja in
Gwagwalada, Nile and Baze in the Municipal Area Council — so browsing by
LGA only ever shows you a fraction of them. Search by name and join each
one you belong to.
:::

## Primary vs. active community

CivicOS distinguishes two things:

- **Primary community** (called your _home community_ during onboarding)
  — this is where you can **create** issues, petitions, and (if you have
  the right role) representative profiles. Pick the place you're most
  likely to actually raise something.
- **Active community** — the community you're currently viewing and
  acting in for signatures, comments, and upvotes.

Most of the time these are the same. They diverge when you visit another
community's page — you can still sign petitions and comment there, but
you can't create new issues outside your primary community.

## Joining another community

You can be a member of multiple communities. To join another:

1. Go to **Communities** in the top nav.
2. Search by name, LGA, or state.
3. Click **Join community**.

Joining doesn't change your primary — it just adds a new membership.
Being a member is what lets you comment, upvote, and sign petitions in
that community.

## Switching your active community

Use the community picker in the top nav to switch between communities
you've joined. Your feed, notifications badge, and the "act" buttons on
representative and issue pages all follow the active community.

## Changing your primary community

If you actually move — new city, new LGA — you can change your primary
community.

1. Go to **Profile → Community**.
2. Click **Change primary community**.
3. Pick the target (you must have already joined it).

Choosing your home during onboarding does **not** burn this allowance —
the cooldown starts from that first choice, so your next change is free
whenever you need it.

**One caveat:** you can only change your primary community **once every
30 days**. This is deliberate — it stops people bouncing between
communities to stack signatures or file issues in places they don't
actually live. If you're within the 30-day cooldown, the UI shows the
next date you can change.
